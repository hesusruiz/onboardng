# Design Token System: High-Density Operational Tool

## Global Principles
- Tone: Serious, professional, strict utility. Zero decorative elements, zero gradients, small rounded shapes.
- Data Priority: Values must always be bold and prominent; structural labels must be clear but secondary. Section headers must be visible but not distracting from the content in the sections.
- Component Style: Non coloured containers with high-contrast sharp borders. No dynamic effects on the sections or the data. Hovering effects are allowed only in buttons, and never distracting the user, just providing useful operational information. 
- Content: All content is to be in structured format, with clear hierarchy and organization. No free text or unstructured data.

## Colors
- --bg-canvas: #F4F5F7 (Light slate gray for optimal backdrop separation)
- --bg-card: #FFFFFF (Pure crisp white container fill)
- --border-primary: #1A1A1A (Sharp, explicit outline for section definition)
- --border-secondary: #E2E8F0 (Subtle separator lines inside sections)
- --text-main: #000000 (Maximum contrast for operational readings)
- --text-muted: #5A6A85 (To prevent visual noise over structural labels)

## Spacing & Layout
- Card Margin/Gap: 16px
- Inner Card Padding: 16px
- Layout Model: Multi-column responsive CSS Grid layouts
